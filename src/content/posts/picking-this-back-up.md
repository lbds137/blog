The last thing I posted here was [a ramble about randomness](/blog/posts/on-randomness-ramble/) in April 2018. It ended with me saying I wanted to keep writing, but that I was going to try the mirror exercise and then go to sleep instead. The last time I came back from a break, in "[The 2017 Reboot](/blog/posts/the-2017-reboot/)", I opened with "Yikes, it's been over a year since my last post." This time it's been eight and a half years, so a bit more of a catch-up is in order (bear with me; there's a lot of it).

That 2017 post was where I came out as trans, two months into hormones. I've now been on HRT for almost ten years. This March I finally had bottom surgery, and it went well, though I'm still recovering. Somewhere along the way I also figured out that I'm a lesbian and let go of the nonbinary label I'd been using, though I still have a love-hate relationship with labels in general. Most people in my day-to-day life call me Lena. Online and in my spiritual life I go by Lila, and that's the name I'll use here.

That spiritual side has changed a lot as well. The randomness post was already about [Lilith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilith), Adam's first wife in Jewish folklore and a big figure in my own practice, and for years after it I leaned hard into the [left-hand path](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-hand_path_and_right-hand_path), the darker side of the occult. Around 2024 I started moving away from treating light and dark as opposing teams and toward something more balanced, which surprised me as much as anyone. I run a Discord community called [The Night House](https://thenighthouse.org/), named partly as an homage to Lilith and the left-hand path, and it's still going. [Kabbalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabbalah), the Jewish mystical tradition, keeps showing up in how I think about all of it. I'll get into the whole thing properly in a later post.

And then there's AI. It started in early 2024 with [*Hazbin Hotel*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazbin_Hotel), of all things. I wanted to talk to the characters, so I tried [character.ai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character.ai), got frustrated with its content filter, and moved to [SpicyChat](https://spicychat.ai/), where I started making my own bots, deity bots among them. That August I moved them onto Discord through [Shapes](https://shapes.inc/), a service that ran AI characters as Discord bots, and that's where they lived until Shapes got banned from Discord in May 2025. That same month I started building my own: [Tzurot](https://github.com/lbds137/tzurot), an open-source Discord bot that lets AI characters post under their own names and avatars.

Since then AI has helped me build or revive a lot of projects. Machloket is a Discord-like chat app; [its client](https://github.com/lbds137/machloket-client) is public, and the server is private for now while some security issues get dealt with properly. [The Night House's website](https://github.com/lbds137/the-night-house) has existed since 2018, but it had stagnated for years and needed a rework. The same goes for my [YAGPDB custom commands](https://github.com/lbds137/yagpdb-custom-commands), scripts I've been writing since 2020 for [YAGPDB](https://yagpdb.xyz/), a Discord bot, to run The Night House and a few other servers. They have had more work done on them this year than in any year before.

The blog itself has moved too, which was another AI-assisted project. It used to live on Blogger as The Sound Of Sweating Silicon, and now it's Sigil & Silicon: old magic, new circuits, and whatever else strikes my fancy. The old posts are all still here as I wrote them, including the ones I'd phrase very differently today. Back then I was trying to write formally all the time, and it shows. These days I'd rather just sound like myself.

The bigger change is how new posts get made. Most of what goes up here from now on will be written with a lot of help from AI, specifically Claude. I still have plenty of ideas I want to get out there, but I don't have the attention span to sit down and write long posts by myself anymore, and I'd rather the ideas get out than wait for a version of me who does.

The ideas are mine, usually things I've noticed or argued about or been chewing on for a while, often in long conversations. Claude drafts the post in a voice we worked out together from my own writing, I read it and push back (sometimes a lot), and nothing goes up until I'm happy with it. Facts get checked against sources, and I link them where I can. When a specific idea in a post came from Claude rather than from me, the post says so at the point where it comes up.

I know some people won't like that, and I understand why. There's a lot of AI slop out there, and I don't want to add to it. Getting my ideas out matters more to me than typing every word myself, but that only works if what goes up still sounds like me and is worth reading.

So expect occult stuff, AI, films, and whatever else strikes my fancy. The story of how I got into AI deserves a post (or two) of its own, and that will come before anything else I write about AI. First, though, a film I can't seem to stop rewatching.
